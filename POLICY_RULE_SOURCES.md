# Policy Rule Sources

The current recommendation rules are prototype operational suggestions based on project-monitoring best practices. They are not presented as CVC, NITI Aayog, MoSPI, or other official policy instructions because the repository does not contain verified rule-to-source citations with section/page URLs.

Each trigger is derived from observed telemetry (for example, a progress/expenditure gap, high expenditure velocity with stalled progress, or reported schedule slippage). Administrative users must obtain the applicable departmental rule, sanction authority, contract clause, or audit guidance before acting.
